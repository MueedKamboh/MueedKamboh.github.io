About

Where power electronics
meets grid operations.

My path started at the hardware level — designing converters, battery management systems and inverter control loops. Over the years it widened to the system level: how storage behaves on a real network, how it earns money in energy markets, and how dozens of assets can be steered as one.

Today I work as a Senior Energy Engineer for Asset Integration & Operation at E.ON, onboarding battery storage and distributed assets into the virtual power plant and keeping them available around the clock. Before that I led grid-forming control development for utility-scale storage at Fluence and drove BESS innovation and patent work at E.ON Group Innovation.

What I enjoy most is the translation layer: turning a market schedule into an inverter setpoint that respects the physics, the grid code and the battery's health.
